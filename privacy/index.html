Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Workout Battle handles information when you create an account, record workouts, join battles or use optional verification features.

Effective 6 September 2026 · Version 1.2

1. Scope

Workout Battle (“Workout Battle”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is an adults-only fitness tracking and competition app. This policy applies to the iOS and Apple Watch apps and the services used to support them.

Workout Battle does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for cross-app advertising or tracking.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile

Your Sign in with Apple identifier and, when you choose to provide them, your name, username, email address and profile image.

Fitness and app activity

Workout exercises, sets, repetitions, weight, duration, distance, training plans, goals, progress, battle participation, scores and app settings. Height, weight, date of birth and related measurements are protected locally using an app encryption key.

Device and diagnostics

Notification preferences and a device notification token may be used to deliver app notifications. If you opt in to analytics, Workout Battle keeps a limited diagnostic event trail on your device; this release does not transmit analytics to a third-party analytics provider.

User content

An optional profile image and any proof video you deliberately record for a verified battle set. Camera access is requested only when you use a camera feature.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate your account, provide workout tracking and history, calculate progress and scores, operate friend battles, count repetitions in verified sets, show relevant reminders, maintain security, prevent abuse, provide support and respond to access or deletion requests.

4. Storage and sharing

Most personal workout history is stored on your protected device. If you enable private workout backup, encrypted workout configuration and completed-session history are stored in your private Apple CloudKit database. Body measurements, heart-rate data, HealthKit imports and proof content are excluded from that private backup.

Profile directory information, minimal battle state and proof needed for a friend battle use Apple CloudKit. Battle records are shared only with the people who accept the private invitation. A public username directory may be used so other users can find the username you choose. Apple processes CloudKit, Sign in with Apple, push notification and HealthKit information under Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Proof videos

Proof recording is optional and is used to help count repetitions and allow the accepted rival to review a verified set. Record only yourself and avoid bystanders or private information in the frame.

The clip is shared only inside its private battle. It is designed to be removed when the rival finishes watching it or no later than 48 hours after submission when either participant next connects. Cancelled and stale temporary recordings are removed automatically. A small set result—such as exercise, repetitions, duration and confidence—may remain for scoring after the video is removed.

6. Apple Health and location

Apple Health access is optional. With your permission, Workout Battle can read workouts you select and can save eligible workouts you complete in the app. Health data is used only for fitness functionality and is not used for advertising.

When you start supported outdoor cardio tracking and grant permission, the app uses location to calculate distance and draw your route, including while that workout is active in the background. Route and location information remain in your local workout record or an Apple Health workout route when you choose to save it; they are not placed in public or battle records.

7. Retention

  • Proof video follows the 48-hour rule described above.
  • Pending invitations expire after 14 days.
  • Inactive battle records are removed after 60 days.
  • A minimal completed battle summary may remain for up to 30 days so both devices can archive it locally.
  • Your selected one-, two- or five-year history setting applies to applicable local workout, audit and completed-battle records.
  • Some settings remain until you change them, delete your account or remove the app.

Retention cleanup that relies on an online service occurs when a participant next connects, so deletion may not be instantaneous while every participant is offline.

8. Your choices and rights

Profile and privacy settings let you change optional permissions, export your data, reset workout information and delete your account. Account deletion removes local information, encryption keys, cached proof, owned CloudKit battles and your uploaded proof from battles owned by other people. An iCloud connection may be required to confirm deletion of shared records.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need enough information to verify that the request concerns your account.

9. Security

Workout Battle uses iOS Data Protection, Keychain, CryptoKit, private CloudKit sharing and data minimisation. No system is completely secure. If a security incident affects your information, we will provide notices where required by applicable law.

10. Children

Workout Battle is intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. Material changes will be identified by a new effective date and may require renewed acceptance in the app.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or rights requests, email support@workoutbattle.com.
